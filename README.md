Overview :-

This project analyzes student depression patterns using Tableau and SQL(GROUP BY, CASE WHEN, JOINS, WINDOWS FUNCTION)

The goal was to identify major factors affecting student mental health such as:

1)Academic pressure

2)Sleep duration

3)Study satisfaction

4)Gender-wise depression trends

The dashboard helps understand which student groups are at higher risk of depression and what lifestyle or academic factors are strongly connected to mental health issues.

Problem :-

Student depression has become a major issue in colleges and universities.

Many students experience :-

1)High academic pressure

2)Poor sleep schedules

3)Low study satisfaction

4)Mental stress

However, it is difficult to identify :-

1)Which students are more affected.

2)What factors contribute most to depression.

3)Which category of students are high-risk.

The project aims to solve this problem through data visualization and analysis.

Key Insights :-

1)50% students are depressed.

2)Male students slightly higher depression(%).

3)High pressure + low sleep = highest risk group.

4)Low study satisfaction → more depression.

5)Students with higher study satisfaction scores (4–5) showed comparatively lower depression rates, suggesting that improved academic satisfaction positively influences overall mental well-being.

Conclusion:-

The KPI cards highlight that over 50% students are affected, establishing the seriousness.
The gender analysis identifies higher risk among male students.
Most importantly, the heatmap combines academic pressure and sleep data to reveal that students with high pressure and low sleep are the most vulnerable group.
Study satisfaction analysis shows behavioral patterns contributing to depression.
Students with higher study satisfaction scores (4–5) showed comparatively lower depression rates, suggesting that improved academic satisfaction positively influences overall mental well-being.
